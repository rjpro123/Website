Frequently Asked Questions

How much does lawn mowing cost?

It depends on the size of your yard and how overgrown it is. Most standard Adelaide suburban lawns fall between $40 and $80 per visit. Send us a couple of photos and we will give you a firm quote.

Do I need to be home when you come?

No. As long as we have access to the yard and your payment details, we can do the job while you are out. Just tell us where the gate or key is.

How often do I need my lawn mowed?

During spring and summer, most Adelaide lawns need mowing every 1-2 weeks. In winter, every 3-4 weeks is usually enough. We can set up a regular schedule so you never have to think about it.

Do you clean up after the job?

Yes. We blow down driveways and paths, clear clippings, and leave the yard tidy. If you want clippings left on the lawn for mulch, just let us know.

What if I am not happy with the work?

Tell us straight away and we will come back and fix it. No fuss, no charge for the re-visit.

Can you handle large or overgrown yards?

Yes. We have the right gear for big jobs and overgrown mess. Send photos and we will quote it honestly, including any extra cost for heavy cleanup.
