Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?

We offer lawn mowing, hedging and pruning, weed control and spraying, garden cleanups, planting and garden beds, pre-sale property preparation, turf replacement, mulching, tree work, and soil aeration. See the cards above for full details.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your yard and the work needed. Most standard Adelaide suburban lawns fall between $40 and $80 per mow. Send us photos and we will give you a firm quote within a day.

Do you do one-off jobs or only regular service?

Both. We are happy to do a one-off cleanup or set up a regular fortnightly or monthly schedule. Whatever works for you.

What areas do you cover?

We service Adelaide's south, west and surrounding suburbs including Marion, Brighton, Glenelg, Unley, Stirling and Norwood. Not sure? Send us a message.

Are you insured?

Yes, we are fully insured. Your property is in safe hands while we work.
